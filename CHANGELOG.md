# Changelog

## 0.1.1

- Loads on any NeoForge (or Forge) build of its Minecraft version, not only the newest: 0.1.0 asked for the
  newest and would not load in packs on older ones (All the Mods 10's NeoForge 21.1.250, for one).

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-15)

- First release, for NeoForge 26.3, 26.1.2 and 1.21.1 and Forge 1.20.1: the tick scan, the frame scan and the chunk
  inspector, with the results on the world and in the results screen, lag spikes and stutters, reports to share, a HUD
  line and commands for servers.
